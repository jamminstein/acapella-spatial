Acapella Spatial

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Acapella Spatial is a spatial vocal looper for Apple Vision Pro. It is designed to run entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app touches and what it does with it.

  • Your recordings stay local unless you choose to export or share them.
  • No automatic uploads or cloud sync. The app has no server.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Microphone

Acapella Spatial records your voice through the microphone so you can capture singing and humming as spatial loops. Audio is captured and processed entirely on-device. The app does not upload recordings automatically. You decide whether to export or share a copy. Recordings you keep are saved locally as audio files inside the app's own Documents area. You can delete clips and scenes from within the app; they are also removed when you delete the app.

Hand tracking

The app uses hand tracking to let you capture and shape sounds in the air (pinch, drag, and stretch gestures). Hand-tracking data is used live, on-device, to drive these interactions. It is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Data storage

Your captured clips and saved scenes are stored locally on your Vision Pro. The app does not automatically sync them or send them to us. Deleting the app removes its local recordings and scenes, so keep a scene backup if you want to restore them later. Deleting a clip or the app does not delete copies you have exported to another location.

Exporting, sharing and importing

You can choose to export an audio mix, separate audio tracks, or a scene backup. A scene backup includes your original recordings and the settings and positions needed to reopen the room. Files are prepared on your device. You choose where to save or share them through the system file or sharing controls.

A location or app you choose, such as a cloud storage provider, may transfer or sync that copy under its own privacy policy. Acapella does not send a copy to the developer. Importing a scene backup reads the file you select and adds a new saved scene on your device.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data or receive recordings through the app. Sharing an exported file or emailing a recording to support is your choice.

Children's privacy

Acapella Spatial does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Hearing and volume

Acapella Spatial plays sound through your headset. Layered loops, effects and the demo can become loud, and spatial audio can feel closer than it is. The app includes a limiter and an automatic loudness guard, but no software can know what is safe for your ears.

You are responsible for your own listening volume. Start low, keep your headset volume moderate, and take breaks. Stop immediately if you experience discomfort, ringing, or pain. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the developer accepts no liability for hearing damage, discomfort, or any other harm arising from listening volume or from the audio the app produces.

Contact

For privacy questions, email acapellavp@icloud.com. If you contact us, your email and anything you choose to attach are used to respond to your request. Sending us an attachment is optional and separate from saving or exporting a scene.
