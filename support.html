Acapella Spatial

Support

Acapella Spatial is a spatial vocal looper for Apple Vision Pro. Sing, hum, whisper or chant, and each phrase becomes a looping voice you can place around you in space.

Getting started

  • Put the headset on and open the app. A short welcome introduces the basics.
  • Just sing — the app listens for a phrase and captures it on its own.
  • Each phrase becomes a glowing ribbon. Drag one to move it; the sound follows.
  • Tap a ribbon to open its editor and change its mode or effects.

Common questions

It isn't capturing what I sing

The app measures your room for about a second when a session opens, then listens for anything clearly louder than that. If the room is noisy it will be less sensitive. You can always press and hold the microphone button to capture manually.

It captures when I'm not singing

Turn auto-capture off with the toggle in the main window, or tap the live waveform, and use the hold-to-record button instead.

How do I delete a loop?

Drag a ribbon and hold for a moment — a bin appears below it. Drop the ribbon on the bin. If you change your mind, the undo button in the main window brings it back.

How do I start over?

The counter-clockwise arrow in the main window resets the app to its first-run state, including the introduction. It clears your clips, so it asks first.
