Acapella Spatial

Support

Acapella Spatial is a spatial vocal looper for Apple Vision Pro. Sing, hum, whisper or chant, and each phrase becomes a looping voice you can place around you in space.

Download Acapella Spatial on the App Store

Getting started

  • Open the app. One card explains the first phrase; choose “start empty” or “hear a demo”. The room shows the next move as you go.
  • Tap “record a phrase”, sing, then tap “finish recording”. Or pinch and hold the recording button or white ribbon while singing, then release.
  • Each phrase becomes a glowing ribbon. Drag one to move it; the sound follows.
  • Choose “explore sounds” for six room-wide effects, then “open studio” when you want the full controls. In the studio, tap a ribbon to open its editor.
  • “stop sound” stops your loops. In Explore and Studio, tapping a ribbon mutes it in place and tapping again brings it back in time; in Simple a tap stops it. Undo appears when a change can be undone. Going back to a simpler view keeps your recorded loops and their settings.

Common questions

It isn't capturing what I sing

In Simple and Play, tap “record a phrase”, then “finish recording”. Pinching and holding the recording button or white ribbon also works in every view. Hands-free listening is optional and available in the full studio. If you denied microphone access, enable it for Acapella in Settings, then return to the app and choose retry. You can explore the bundled demo without singing.

It captures when I'm not singing

In the studio, turn off hands-free listening and in-air performance, then use the recording button or hold the white ribbon. Going back to a simpler view turns these live recording options off. Start at a low playback volume so the microphone does not pick up loud loops.

How do I delete a loop?

Drag a ribbon and hold for a moment — a bin appears. Drop the ribbon on the bin, or use the delete button in its Studio editor. If you change your mind, the undo button in the main window brings it back.

How do I start over?

In the studio, choose “clear room…” and confirm. The room stays open, and Undo brings your loops back. “start over” is also available under the live ribbon after three loops. Use Scenes to save a named copy first. Saved scenes can be reopened from any level of the main window.

How do I clear the effects?

Under the six Explore sounds controls, choose “clear all effects” to restore the settings you had before using those effects. Your recordings, positions and trim stay in place. The action becomes available after you use an effect.

How do I keep a backup?

In Scenes, open “export & backup”, choose “save a scene backup” and pick a location in Files. The .acapella file keeps your original recordings, their placement and your sound settings. Keep a copy somewhere you can reach if you delete or reinstall the app.

To bring it back, choose “import a scene backup” and select the file. It adds a new saved scene; your current room stays open. Load the imported scene when you’re ready. Existing scenes are kept. To back up a saved scene without opening it, use that scene’s “more actions” menu.

How do I share my sound?

In Scenes, open “export & backup”. Choose 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds, then “stereo mix” for one WAV file or “separate tracks” for individual WAV files. Choose where to save them, then share those files from Files whenever you like.

Each loop starts at the beginning, even if playback is stopped. Your sound settings, mute and solo choices are kept; muted tracks stay silent. Live microphone audio, the live granular instrument and metronome are left out. The export is a fixed stereo view of the room; it doesn’t follow your head.

Use a scene backup to keep the original recordings and editable room as well. You choose whether any exported files leave your headset.

How do I use the deeper hand controls?

Choose “open studio”, then enable “in-air performance” for untargeted left-pinch recording, right-pinch pitch changes, and two-hand volume or reverb. Enable “live granular audio” separately to shape live microphone audio with your right hand. Both options start off; opening more controls does not enable them. Turn live granular audio off to silence that layer.

Hearing and volume

Acapella Spatial plays sound through your headset. Layered loops, effects and the demo can become loud, and spatial audio can feel closer than it is. The app cannot prevent excessive playback volume or determine what is safe for your ears.

You are responsible for your own listening volume. Start low, keep your headset volume moderate, and take breaks. Stop immediately if you experience discomfort, ringing, or pain.
